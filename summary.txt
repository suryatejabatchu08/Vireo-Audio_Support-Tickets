10-Bullet Summary: SLA Targets, Cost Figures, Shift Definitions, and Data Traps

**SLA Targets:**
1. First response SLA targets: Chat 15 minutes, Voice callback 2 hours, Social 4 hours, Email 8 hours (measured from ticket creation to first human agent reply)
2. SLA breach penalty: Automatic Rs 350 store credit issued to customer's account on resolution for any ticket missing its first-response target, charged to the SLA credit line in support P&L

**Cost Figures:**
3. Fully loaded cost per contact: Chat Rs 210, Email Rs 260, Voice callback Rs 520, Social Rs 240; blended average across channel mix: Rs 290 per contact
4. Internal transfer cost: Rs 305 per transfer (covers re-handling and administration when tickets move between teams)
5. Agent labor cost: Fully loaded agent cost of Rs 165 per agent-hour for staffing decisions, with standard shift length of 8 hours

**Shift Definitions:**
6. Shift timings (IST): Morning shift 06:00-14:00, Day shift 14:00-22:00, Night shift 22:00-06:00
7. Roster structure: One row per assignment with from/to dates; agents receive new roster row when changing site/shift but retain same agent_id
8. Queue coverage: Outside staffed hours, coverage is picked up by the next shift in order of creation

**Data Traps:**
9. Timezone inconsistency: Helpdesk API exports timestamps in UTC, while standard reports and shift definitions use Indian Standard Time (IST)
10. Multiple data quality issues: Legacy ticket duplication from re-import, legacy monetary units vs current rupees, reconstructed resolution timestamps for migrated tickets, CSAT scoring inconsistency (legacy 0 vs blank for no response), and ~40 failed IVR transcripts
