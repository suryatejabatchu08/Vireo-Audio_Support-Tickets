Task: Classify the customer message into one of 11 categories.

Categories:
1. Account & Login: Issues with account access, login, OTP, passwords.
2. App & Firmware: Problems with mobile app, software updates, firmware.
3. Audio Quality: Sound issues, microphone, speaker, call quality.
4. Billing & Payments: Charges, payments, refunds, invoices, GST.
5. Charging & Battery: Charging case, battery life, power issues.
6. Connectivity: Bluetooth pairing, connection drops, wireless issues.
7. Delivery & Shipping: Shipping delays, missing items, delivery address.
8. Other: General inquiries, pre-sales questions, non-technical issues.
9. Product Enquiry: Questions about product features, specifications, availability.
10. Returns & Refunds: Return requests, refund status, pickup issues.
11. Warranty & Repair: Repair requests, warranty claims, replacements.

Instructions:
- Read the masked customer message.
- Choose exactly one category from the list above.
- Output the category label on the first line.
- On the second line, provide a brief reason (15 words or less) for your choice.
- If no other category fits, choose 'Other'.

Example output:
Account & Login
Customer mentions login issues and OTP not received.
